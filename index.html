Privacy Policy — Mannat

Applies to the Android app com.fieapps.mannat (“Mannat – Mark Your Wishes | Save Votive”). Last updated 19 November 2026.

The short version

Mannat does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or sensitive user data. The app has no account registration, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. Everything you save in the app stays in its private storage on your device.

Mannat does not require an internet connection to save or access your wishes, prayers or votives. Your saved information remains on your device and is not uploaded to any server or cloud service.

What is stored on your device

Mannat stores the information you choose to add to the app locally in its private storage. This information is never transmitted to us or any third party.

What Why
Your saved wishes To let you record, revisit and manage your personal wishes.
Your prayers and votives To keep a personal record of the prayers and votives you choose to save.
Other information you enter within the app To provide the app's core features and display your saved content.

Data collection and sharing

Mannat does not collect personal information, device identifiers, location information or usage statistics.

We do not operate servers that receive your saved wishes or prayers. We do not sell, rent, share or disclose your personal information to advertisers, analytics providers or other third parties because the app does not collect or transmit such information.

Permissions

Mannat does not require special Android permissions to provide its core functionality. You can save, view and manage your wishes, prayers and votives without granting access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or notifications.

Internet access

Mannat is designed to work offline. It does not require an internet connection to save or access your information, and it does not transmit your saved content to external servers.

Children

Mannat does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The app does not require users to create an account or submit personal information.

Deleting your data

You are in control of the information you save in Mannat. You can delete individual wishes, prayers or votives from within the app, where supported.

Uninstalling Mannat or using Android's "Clear storage" option removes the app's locally stored data.

Since your information is never transmitted to our servers, there is no server-side copy to request deletion of.

Data security

Your saved information is stored in the app's private storage on your device. We do not maintain a remote database or cloud backup of your information.

Please note that the security of locally stored information also depends on the security of your device.

Changes to this policy

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address with a revised "Last updated" date.
